
	* function: Google Play Services API gateway (part of the push notification pipeline)
	
	    * What that means practically:
	    
	    ** When an app on your phone wants to receive push notifications, the developer's server sends the message to Google's FCM servers
	    
	    *** Your device maintains a persistent connection to Google's push infrastructure, and android.apis.google.com serves as an API 
	       access point used by Play Services during this process (registration, token issuance, connection setup)
	    
	    **** Description: It's the bridge between Android system components and Google's backend APIs — most notably as a required component for the 
	         FCM push notification system, plus general Google API access from Play Services (Maps, account sign-in, etc.)
