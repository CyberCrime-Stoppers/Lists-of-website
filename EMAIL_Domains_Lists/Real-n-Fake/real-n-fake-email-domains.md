 ## Fake Email / Fishing Links 
 
 - amazon-orders.com
 - amazonsecurity.net
 - amaz0n-order.com (zero replacing "o")
 - amazon-shipping.info
 #### Patterns to watch: any hyphenated or extra-word variant (amazon-orders.com style), numerals for letters (amaz0n), TLD swaps (amazon-help.net, .info, .co instead of .com)
 
 - apple-id-secure.com
 - apple-id-verify.net
 - apple-support.com (not Apple's)
 - appleinc.co
 - appleid.apple.com-verifyaccount.com style chained domains
 #### Note: noreply@email.apple.com is genuinely disputed — Apple's official rule is mail comes from @apple.com, @icloud.com, or @itunes.com. Since email.apple.com is technically a subdomain Apple does own and    use, treat with headers/DMARC check rather than assuming either way.
